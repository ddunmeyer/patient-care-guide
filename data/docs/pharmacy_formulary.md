# Lakeshore Health PPO: Pharmacy Formulary

Effective January 1, 2026. The formulary is the list of prescription drugs the plan covers. Lakeshore Health PPO is a fictional plan used for demonstration only.

## How Drug Tiers Work

Each covered drug is placed in one of four tiers. Lower tiers cost you less. Your cost depends on the drug's tier and whether you fill it at a retail pharmacy or through mail order. Drug copays do not count toward the medical deductible, but they do count toward the out-of-pocket maximum.

## Drug Tiers and Costs

| Tier | What it includes | Retail (30-day supply) | Mail order (90-day supply) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1: Preferred generic | Most common generic drugs | $10 copay | $20 copay |
| Tier 2: Preferred brand | Brand-name drugs with no generic, chosen for value | $40 copay | $100 copay |
| Tier 3: Non-preferred brand | Brand-name drugs that have a lower-cost alternative | $80 copay | $200 copay |
| Tier 4: Specialty | High-cost drugs for complex conditions | 25% coinsurance, up to $250 per fill | Specialty pharmacy only, 30-day supply |

## Prior Authorization and Step Therapy

- All Tier 4 specialty drugs need prior authorization before the first fill.
- Some Tier 2 and Tier 3 drugs need step therapy: you must try a lower-tier drug first, unless your doctor shows it is not right for you.
- Some drugs have quantity limits, such as a maximum number of pills per month.

## Generic Substitution

When a generic version is available, the pharmacy will fill the generic. If you or your doctor ask for the brand-name drug instead, you pay the Tier 3 copay plus the difference in cost between the brand and the generic.

## Specialty Pharmacy

Tier 4 drugs must be filled through Lakeshore Specialty Pharmacy, which ships to your home and offers nurse support. Call 1-800-555-0166 to set up a first fill.

## Requesting a Drug Not on the Formulary

Your doctor can request a formulary exception if no covered drug works for you. The plan decides standard exception requests within 72 hours and urgent requests within 24 hours.
