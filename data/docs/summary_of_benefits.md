# Lakeshore Health PPO: Summary of Benefits

Plan year: January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026. This summary is a short overview of what the plan covers and what you pay. Lakeshore Health PPO is a fictional plan used for demonstration only.

## Deductible

The deductible is the amount you pay for covered services each plan year before the plan starts to pay.

- In-network: $1,500 per person, $3,000 per family.
- Out-of-network: $3,000 per person, $6,000 per family.
- Preventive care, office visit copays and prescription drug copays do not count toward the deductible and are not subject to it.

## Out-of-Pocket Maximum

The out-of-pocket maximum is the most you pay in a plan year for covered services. After you reach it, the plan pays 100% of covered services for the rest of the year.

- In-network: $6,000 per person, $12,000 per family.
- Out-of-network: $12,000 per person, $24,000 per family.
- Deductible, copays and coinsurance all count toward the out-of-pocket maximum. Premiums do not.

## Office Visit Copays

| Service | In-network | Out-of-network |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary care visit | $25 copay | 40% coinsurance after deductible |
| Specialist visit | $50 copay | 40% coinsurance after deductible |
| Telehealth visit | $10 copay | Not covered |
| Preventive care and annual physical | No charge | 40% coinsurance after deductible |

You do not need a referral to see a specialist.

## Urgent and Emergency Care

| Service | In-network | Out-of-network |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent care center | $75 copay | 40% coinsurance after deductible |
| Emergency room | $350 copay, waived if admitted | $350 copay, waived if admitted |
| Ambulance | 20% coinsurance after deductible | 20% coinsurance after deductible |

Emergency room care is covered at the in-network level even at an out-of-network hospital.

## Lab Work and X-Rays

- Lab tests (blood work, urine tests): no charge in-network; 40% coinsurance after deductible out-of-network.
- X-rays and ultrasounds: 20% coinsurance after deductible in-network; 40% coinsurance after deductible out-of-network.

## MRI and Advanced Imaging

Advanced imaging includes MRI, CT scans, PET scans and nuclear medicine studies.

- Coverage: MRI and other advanced imaging are covered when medically necessary and ordered by a licensed provider.
- In-network cost: 20% coinsurance after deductible, at a freestanding imaging center or hospital outpatient department.
- Out-of-network cost: 40% coinsurance after deductible.
- Prior authorization: required for all non-emergency MRI, CT and PET scans. See the Prior Authorization Policy for how to request it.
- If prior authorization is required and not obtained, the plan may deny the claim, and you may be responsible for the full cost.
- Imaging done in the emergency room during an emergency does not need prior authorization.

Tip: freestanding imaging centers usually cost less than hospital outpatient departments for the same scan.

## Hospital Stays

- Inpatient hospital stay: 20% coinsurance after deductible in-network; 40% coinsurance after deductible out-of-network.
- Outpatient surgery: 20% coinsurance after deductible in-network; 40% coinsurance after deductible out-of-network.
- Planned (non-emergency) hospital admissions need prior authorization.

## Mental Health and Substance Use Services

- Outpatient therapy visit: $25 copay in-network.
- Inpatient mental health or substance use care: 20% coinsurance after deductible in-network. Prior authorization required.

Prescription drug costs depend on the drug tier. See the Pharmacy Formulary for tiers and copays.
